## Security at iFood

iFood operates a broad technology ecosystem serving millions of customers, restaurants, couriers, and partners. Our security teams work across cloud, applications, mobile, identity, incident response, fraud, offensive security, AI, privacy, and security research.

We treat security as an engineering discipline. That means building reliable platforms, measuring real risk, partnering closely with product teams, and designing controls that support innovation rather than slow it down.

## Why we publish

Open-source software and the security community have shaped much of what we know and build. This blog is one way we give back: sharing practical lessons, technical research, tools, architectures, and mistakes from operating security at scale.

Our articles are written by the people doing the work. They reflect real engineering decisions and the context behind them.
